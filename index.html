OUR SERVICES

Corporate Services, Clearly Delivered

Explore our core services for establishing, administering and managing corporate matters in Cyprus.

We provide comprehensive support throughout the company formation process in Cyprus, ensuring that the essential incorporation requirements are handled professionally.

  • Name verification and reservation with the Registrar of Companies.
  • Preparation of the Memorandum and Articles of Association by a Cypriot lawyer.
  • Preparation and submission of HE1, HE2 and HE3 forms, including the sworn statement.
  • Arrangement of the registered office address and company secretary.
  • Application for a Tax Identification Code (TIC) and, where required, VAT registration.
  • UBO registration within the statutory period of 90 days.

We provide ongoing corporate administration and secretarial support, assisting companies in Cyprus with their statutory requirements and corporate records.

  • Preparation of minutes of Directors’ and Shareholders’ Meetings.
  • Maintenance of statutory company records.
  • Conducting company searches at the Registrar of Companies.
  • Preparation and submission of company returns, forms and notifications to the Registrar of Companies, including annual returns and changes in share capital, directors and shareholders.
  • Corporate secretarial services.
  • Registration of trade names and trademarks.

We assist with corporate changes, restructuring matters and company closure procedures in Cyprus, coordinating the required documentation and relevant professional services.

  • Assistance with voluntary strike-off of companies.
  • Coordination of voluntary liquidation procedures.
  • Preparation and coordination of company resolutions.
  • Coordination with licensed insolvency practitioners and legal professionals.
  • Preparation and submission of required forms and notifications to the Registrar of Companies.
  • Follow-up and monitoring of the company closure process.
  • Amendments to the Memorandum of Association.
  • Amendments to the Articles of Association.
WHY CYPRUS

A European Base for Global Ambitions

Cyprus offers an established European business environment, combining a strategic location, a recognised legal framework and an international professional network. It provides a strong foundation for businesses seeking to establish, operate and expand across international markets.

  • European Union MembershipAs an EU Member State and part of the Eurozone, Cyprus offers businesses a European base within the EU regulatory and commercial framework.
  • Strategic Geographic PositionSituated at the crossroads of Europe, the Middle East and Africa, Cyprus offers convenient connections to important international markets.
  • Established Legal FrameworkCyprus combines European legislation with a legal system influenced by English Common Law principles, providing a familiar framework for international business.
  • International Business EnvironmentEnglish is widely used in business, supported by an established network of legal, financial, corporate and professional service providers.
  • Modern Infrastructure & ConnectivityInternational airports, seaports, telecommunications and modern business facilities support companies operating locally and internationally.
